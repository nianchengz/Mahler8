# MISSING FULL TEXT

This file compares bibliographic references in `research_control_original/` with actual full-text files under `sources_raw/`. “Missing” means **not present in the local immutable evidence directory**. It does not mean that a work is unavailable online or through a library. URLs and availability labels below are inherited from the older notes and were not treated as full text.

Repeated mentions of the same work were consolidated. For an edited collection, the absent parent volume covers the individual chapter records listed in the older table of contents; those chapters are not counted again as separate missing files.

## Already matched to local full text

The following older records do have corresponding local full text and are therefore excluded from the missing tables: Batstone 2019 thesis; Devutsky 2016; Goethe's German *Faust II*; Latham's English *Faust I–II*; Mullan Volume 1; Papanikolaou 2017; West 2015.
